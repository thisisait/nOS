# Body plan — one word, one meaning

> **PROPOSED** (2026-10-06, roadmap rows `lexicon` and `prose-retirement`).
> Not in force until the operator says so. The words themselves are already
> ruled in `state/genome/lexicon.yml`; this article says how they are kept.
> Sibling of [`layers.md`](layers.md), which settled the word **tier** the same way.

## 1. Why one meaning

nOS names its parts after a living body. When one word carries two meanings,
a model reading the repo learns whichever it read first, and then acts on it
with confidence. Measured 2026-10-05: **organ** meant four different sets,
**memory** meant three, and KEAP was also called the cortex.

So each word has exactly one meaning, written down once:

- **Source:** `state/genome/lexicon.yml` — the meaning, the level, the places
  the word lives in the repo, and the senses it no longer carries.
- **Rendered:** [`docs/glossary.md`](../../docs/glossary.md), generated by
  `tools/genome-codegen.py`. Never edited by hand.
- **Gate:** `tests/anatomy/test_lexicon_holds.py`. It checks every place the
  lexicon names exists, every retired place is gone, every node kind of the
  anatomy graph has one level, and that retired phrases do not appear in the
  files models are told to trust.
- **Projection:** `tools/body.py` shows the estate by level. It reads
  `state/body-plan.json`, which `tools/body-plan-gen.py` builds from the
  anatomy graph and the lexicon; there is no second map.

## 2. How to add or change a word

1. Write the entry in `state/genome/lexicon.yml`: `level`, one plain
   sentence in `means`, the `names` where it lives, and `not` for the words
   it must not be confused with.
2. To retire a sense, add it under `retired` with its `replacement`, and add
   the exact phrases a model must stop being taught to `retired_phrases`.
3. Run `python3 tools/genome-codegen.py` to render the glossary, then
   `--check`.
4. Run the gate. Today's leftovers sit in its `pending` lists, which may only
   shrink: fixing one fails the gate until its line is deleted.

A new word the operator has not ruled carries `proposed: true`.

## 3. The levels, smallest to largest

| level | one meaning | lives in |
|---|---|---|
| genome | the declared facts every part inherits | `state/genome/` |
| cell | one model in one specialization (in code: an agent) | `files/anatomy/agents/` |
| tissue | the transplantable pack of one specialization: its cells, skills, tables, services and reflexes | `state/tissues/` |
| organ | one service or host daemon with one job; one row | `state/manifest.yml` |
| organ system | a public group of organs serving one function | the apex ruling's `organ_systems:` key |
| organism | one nOS install on one machine | — |
| habitat | the machine and what lives beside the organism | `software_owner` |

A cell's own prompt and tool allow-list is its **definition**. A genome
entity kind is a **gene**.

## 4. Words that cross the levels

- **self / symbiont / habitat** — who wrote the software and who updates it.
- **declared / undeclared** — on the generated list or not; the immune
  system tolerates the first and reports the second.
- **sense** reads, **limb** acts. An immune indicator is a sense with an
  indicator contract ([`immune-system.md`](immune-system.md) §2).
- **memory** is what nOS has learned, kept in KEAP. **verdicts** is the log
  of what each sense reported and what the operator judged.
- **law** is the rules in force (`ssot/doctrine/`); it is kept apart from the genome.
- **Pulse** is the scheduler; one scheduled job is a **reflex**. A
  **heartbeat** is a signal that proves something is still alive.
- **nervous system** — events → Wing inbox → ntfy or mail. One playbook run
  is a **converge**.
- **plugin** — one `plugin.yml`; what it declares is **plugin wiring**.
- **digest** — the process that turns outside data into DataTable rows.
- **Cortex** is the reasoning machinery, not KEAP. The first reader of a
  session is `tools/red-status.py`. **twin** is the second Mac; two lists
  that must stay equal are in **mirror-parity**.

The glossary's last section lists every retired sense with what to say now.

## 5. The four old meanings of organ, and where each went

| old meaning | where it went |
|---|---|
| the named host parts (Bone, Wing, Pulse, Cortex) | each is an **organ**: a host daemon with one job and one manifest row |
| runs on the host, not in a container | the **stack** axis: `stack: null` in `state/manifest.yml` |
| the KEAP taxonomy prefix `nos.host.*` | stays as spelled; a legacy anchor prefix for host-native organs |
| a group of services shown to strangers on the apex page | **organ system** |

Two later meanings were retired the same day: a model is a **cell**, not an
organ; nOS's own parts, as against vendor software, are **self** organs.
