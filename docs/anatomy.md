# Anatomy of nOS

nOS names its parts after a living body, and each word has one meaning.

- **What each word means:** [`glossary.md`](glossary.md), rendered from
  `state/genome/lexicon.yml`.
- **How the words are kept, and the levels from genome to habitat:**
  [`ssot/doctrine/body-plan.md`](../ssot/doctrine/body-plan.md).
- **What the estate looks like by level, right now:** `python3 tools/body.py`
  (or `tools/body.py service:bone` for one part).
- **Ports, launchd labels and install flags:** ask `state/manifest.yml` or
  `tools/estate-status.py`, not a page.
